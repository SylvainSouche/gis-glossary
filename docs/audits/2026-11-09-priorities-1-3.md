files for each language, and the Geolexica concept YAML. Coverage in the

## Findings

- **Nynorsk has no source so far.** GEMET's `no` tag is Bokmål on spelling
  evidence: 186 labels with *vann*, none with *vatn*.
- **GEMET covers languages, not GIS.** It is environmental, and definitions
  exist in depth only for English, Bulgarian, Slovenian and Polish.
- **INSPIRE files fall back to English.** Only entries whose label is tagged
  with the requested language are translations.
- **ISO MLGT is the GIS core but has no stated reuse licence.** Copyright is
  given as ISO/TC 211, and it has no Italian or Tier 3 language other than
  Swedish. The "freely available" wording in the TMG README is not a licence.
- **Licences are general notices.** The GEMET and INSPIRE CC BY 4.0 findings
  come from the EEA and Europa legal notices, not dataset-specific terms.

## Decision after this audit

ISO MLGT term equivalents may be used in the glossary (`dec-mlgt-terms-usable`).
The raw files and definitions remain restricted.

## Not checked

GIS relevance of GEMET concepts, translation quality, how far the three sources
overlap, and EU database rights in compiled term sets.
