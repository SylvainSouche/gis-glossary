# Source audit: final pass

Audited 2026-11-09.

## New useful sources

| Source | Size | Languages | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Realfagstermer | About 10,000 labels each | Bokmål, Nynorsk, English | CC0 |
| Rikstermbanken open part | Not counted | Multilingual, Swedish-centred | CC0 |
| ZRC geoinformatics dictionary | Over 500 entries across publications | Slovenian | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 |

## Closed

Agency home pages with no glossary were closed with a pointer to the real source.
Hugging Face datasets, Glosario, GIS Pantry and the GDAL glossary add no useful
language coverage. Standards bodies and Esri are reference-only. The standards
bodies were classified by role, without reading each one's terms.

## PostGIS manual translations

Finished strings of about 6,065: Swedish 6,058; French 4,523; German 2,269;
Italian 1,075; Spanish 1,004; Polish 133; Romanian 15; Danish 9.

## Still open

EuroVoc and EU Vocabularies licences, IATE counts (EU Login), LÍSA and Slovak
dictionary reuse terms, QGIS.bg licence, Rikstermbanken GIS content, the Icelandic
Translation Centre, the Målfrid corpus, the GRASS `.po` files, the Greek paper,
and the EUR-Lex acts.
