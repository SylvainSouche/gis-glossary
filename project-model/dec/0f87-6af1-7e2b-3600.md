# A terminology glossary (term equivalents with their sense) is not a derived work and can be published independently under a Creative Commons licence; a corpus including literal excerpts must keep the source licence or a compatible one; they are separate artefacts, and every glossary row records its source, address, source licence, method and retrieval date
